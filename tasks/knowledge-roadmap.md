# Knowledge delivery roadmap

This document owns delivery order and routes the work. Individual [tasks](index.md) own status and dependencies.
[Knowledge content](../docs/product-specs/knowledge-content.md) owns scope.
[Knowledge model](../docs/design-docs/knowledge-model.md) owns structure and loading.
[Knowledge quality](../docs/product-specs/knowledge-quality.md) owns acceptance and source review.

## Sequence

1. [Feat-068](task-feat-068.md): simplify knowledge contracts and improve quẻ 01-04 and their 24 positions.
2. [Feats 069](task-feat-069.md)-[083](task-feat-083.md): review the remaining four-quẻ groups under the [hexagram batch policy](../docs/product-specs/knowledge-quality.md#hexagram-review-batches).
3. [Feats 084](task-feat-084.md)-[093](task-feat-093.md): review the assigned Liu Yao and classical topic groups; preserve meaningful tables and source differences.
4. [Feat-094](task-feat-094.md): resolve material content gaps, contradictions, and broken links.
5. [Feats 095](task-feat-095.md)-[096](task-feat-096.md): optional editorial and learning-readiness passes, only when selected; no certification program.
6. [Feats 098](task-feat-098.md)-[100](task-feat-100.md): expose detailed quẻ/hào, references, and linked topics/articles in the web app.
7. [Feats 102](task-feat-102.md)-[103](task-feat-103.md): check rendering, payload, and offline behavior as those flows change.

Web integration does not wait for corpus-wide completion. Preserve each task's explicit start gates.
Independent approved tasks can run concurrently; coordinate overlapping record or source-file ownership before editing.
Selection never follows automatically from a previous task's next-action suggestion.

## Working unit

Review one coherent record or source/topic group at a time.
A quẻ checklist covers the overview, six positions, and meaningful special passages.
A topic checklist covers its actual concepts, examples, and tables.
Record material findings and one concrete next action in the selected task.
Missing or unclear source text stays explicit and does not block unrelated records.

The [publication gate](../docs/product-specs/knowledge-quality.md#publication-gate) governs authored content.
Do not resume the superseded machine-audit, hash-revision, or certification program.
Historical completion counts and retained [legacy evidence](../features/README.md) are not new authoring templates or proof of source fidelity.
