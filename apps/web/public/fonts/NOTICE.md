# Font asset notices

The WOFF2 files in this directory are modified subsets of the upstream fonts listed in `README.md`, generated from the exact pinned Google Fonts commit recorded there. All are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The complete upstream OFL texts, including each font's copyright line, are included alongside the assets: `Noto-OFL.txt` (Latin Noto Sans and Noto Serif), `Noto-Serif-CJK-OFL.txt` (Noto Serif SC), `Noto-Sans-CJK-OFL.txt` (Noto Sans SC), and `Cinzel-OFL.txt` (Cinzel).

The Noto faces were subsetted to Latin/Vietnamese coverage or the 96 app CJK codepoints in `cjk-coverage.txt`. The verbatim upstream notices are: “Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors (https://github.com/notofonts/latin-greek-cyrillic)” for Latin Noto; “Copyright 2012 Google Inc. All Rights Reserved.” for Noto Serif SC; “Copyright 2014-2021 Adobe (http://www.adobe.com/), with Reserved Font Name 'Source'” for Noto Sans SC; and “Copyright 2020 The Cinzel Project Authors (https://github.com/NDISCOVER/Cinzel)” for Cinzel. Cinzel was subsetted to Latin characters and renamed internally to **Liuyao Cinzel** / **Liuyao-Cinzel** to respect the OFL Reserved Font Name. These files are not the original unmodified upstream fonts. Full upstream OFL texts and notices are retained in the corresponding files above.
