# Font asset notices

The WOFF2 files in this directory are modified subsets of the upstream fonts listed in `README.md`, generated from the exact pinned Google Fonts commit recorded there. All are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the complete upstream license texts are included as `Noto-OFL.txt` and `Cinzel-OFL.txt`.

The Noto faces were subsetted to Latin/Vietnamese coverage or the eight listed Han characters. Cinzel was subsetted to Latin characters and renamed internally to **Liuyao Cinzel** / **Liuyao-Cinzel** to respect the OFL Reserved Font Name. These files are not the original unmodified upstream fonts. Upstream authors and license notices remain in the accompanying OFL files.
