# Supplied-book source inventory

This crosswalk owns source section → existing record, uncovered work, exclusion or non-content disposition, and planned author/audit owner. It classifies all 2,453 PDF pages. It is not a passage-fidelity audit, a specialist review, or a certification. The planned machine projection and decision rules are owned by the [knowledge-model audit contract](../../design-docs/knowledge-model.md#approved-versioned-audit-contract); this crosswalk remains the source-unit authority.

Edition identity, fingerprints, bibliography, rights, and discrepancy resolutions remain in [Book sources](../../references/book-sources.md) and [`sources.json`](../../../packages/knowledge/data/sources.json). Corpus claims and release requirements remain in [Knowledge quality](../../product-specs/knowledge-quality.md). Stable record and citation contracts remain in [Knowledge model](../../design-docs/knowledge-model.md). The roadmap routes all planned authoring and audit features in [Knowledge roadmap](../../../features/knowledge-roadmap.md).

## Reading the dispositions

| Label                                 | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Selected coverage; remainder open** | Released records or citations support selected claims only. They do not cover the whole source unit or its actual layers.                                                     |
| **Uncovered**                         | The source material is present, but no matching authored record/citation was found in the current manifest crosswalk. It is not excluded.                                     |
| **Source-reported omission**          | The supplied edition explicitly marks material missing, reduced, untranslated, or represented elsewhere. Inventory the extant statement/text; do not reconstruct absent text. |
| **Unlocated; unresolved**             | A source reference or attribution target was not found. Keep it visible; do not claim source-wide absence.                                                                    |
| **Non-content disposition**           | A cover, divider, blank, contents page, plate, or colophon has an explicit navigation/editorial disposition, not a doctrinal claim.                                           |
| **Pending owner review**              | The source unit remains included in the work queue. Finer passage, image, attribution, or claim decisions belong to its named feature.                                        |

PDF pages are one-based and include covers. Printed labels are a separate evidence field. An extraction match is not an image-verified printed label. A blank heading search is not evidence of source absence. The exclusive ranges below partition pages; nested figures, lines, headings, and shared-page locators are child units and never change those ranges.
