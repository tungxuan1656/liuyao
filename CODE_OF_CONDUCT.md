# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors must keep project spaces respectful, professional, and focused on the work.

Examples of acceptable behavior include constructive technical disagreement, clear feedback, and respect for different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate disruption, or publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They can edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate them.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer. Do not post sensitive reports in a public issue.

Maintainers must handle reports as confidentially as practical and avoid conflicts of interest when reviewing them.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and in public spaces when someone is officially representing the project.
